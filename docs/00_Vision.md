# RouteCare AI

Version: 1.0

## Vision

RouteCare AI is an intelligent scheduling and route optimization platform built specifically for home healthcare providers.

The platform helps therapists, physical therapist assistants (PTAs), occupational therapists, speech therapists, nurses, and home healthcare agencies reduce time spent driving by automatically recommending the most efficient patient schedules.

Unlike traditional scheduling software that focuses only on appointment times, RouteCare AI considers geographic location, therapist availability, patient availability, travel time, visit duration, recurring visits, and route efficiency simultaneously.

The goal is to help clinicians spend more time treating patients and less time behind the wheel.

## Problem Statement

Home healthcare scheduling is largely manual.

Therapists often schedule visits based on memory or availability rather than geographic efficiency.

This results in:

- Excessive driving
- Lost productivity
- Increased fuel costs
- Therapist burnout
- Fewer patients seen each day

Even experienced therapists struggle to manually optimize schedules while accounting for patient availability and travel time.

---

## Solution

RouteCare AI continuously analyzes therapist calendars, patient locations, visit durations, recurring appointments, and travel routes to recommend the best schedule.

When a new patient needs to be scheduled, the platform identifies:

- Best therapist
- Best day
- Best appointment time
- Best visit sequence
- Estimated travel time
- Estimated mileage
- Schedule efficiency score

The therapist remains in control and can accept, modify, or reject recommendations.
